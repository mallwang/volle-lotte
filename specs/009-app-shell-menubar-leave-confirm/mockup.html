App shell: menubar and leave confirmation

Click the entries in the menubar to move between pages. In a lobby or a running game the leave dialog opens. The game table, dice, log and open-games list are plain stand-ins; they do not change in this feature.

What to look at

  • Forms (nickname, create game) and the lobby and result pages are centred in a narrow column. Menubar and pages are limited to 1200 px and centred; try "Wide 1600".
  • The open page is highlighted by fill, underline and weight, not by colour alone, and carries aria-current="page".
  • On the Nickname page the page links are disabled; brand, language and theme still work.
  • Leave dialog: "Stay" is the default (focus and Escape). Texts differ for a running game, a host lobby and a guest lobby. The result page leaves without a dialog.
  • The theme button shows the current mode (sun or moon) and switches between light and dark only.

Open question shown here

  • The "Current game" entry. Leaving a game always ends it, so the entry is only ever visible on the game page and always highlighted. Switch to a lobby or the running game to see it.

Not drawn

  • Real icons (the real app uses PrimeIcons sun and moon), the German texts, keyboard behaviour of the menubar, and the table itself.
