Questions for the review

  • Position: the sound button sits between the language select and the theme button. Fine, or after the theme button?
  • Icon: speaker with waves when on, speaker with a cross when muted (PrimeIcons pi-volume-up / pi-volume-off in the app). The icon shows the current state; the tooltip says what a click does.
  • Texts: "Sounds: on. Mute sounds" / "Turn sounds on" and German "Töne: an. Töne stummschalten" / "Töne einschalten". Switch the language above to see them in place.

Fidelity

Colours are the real Terracotta tokens; the theme button switches the canvas to dark. The menubar, seats and dice are simplified stand-ins for the PrimeNG menubar (spec 009) and the table (spec 007). The file names are a proposal for the plan.
