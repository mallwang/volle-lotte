      What to look at

      • Three zones in landscape: all seats (left), card, turn points and dice (centre), actions (right, thumb side). Nothing scrolls.
      • The active seat is filled with a thick border and an arrow, not colour alone. With 8 seats each row is about 33 px; names are shortened with an ellipsis, scores never.
      • Dice are 46-56 px with 6-8 px gaps; the Leave button has a 44 px hit area. Buttons are narrow, labels wrap onto two lines, 48 px high.
      • Disabled actions explain themselves in the hint line under the buttons (minimum points, set aside first).
      • Results and info text (bust, Volle Lotte, Street) sit in the free space above the buttons, so card, turn points and dice stay visible. Thin lines separate seats, centre and actions.
      • Portrait shows only the blocker with the state of the turn and Leave game; the table state is kept in the background.
      • Out of this mockup: sounds, log, rematch, reconnect banner, spectators (M3); animation details (shown as static faces).
