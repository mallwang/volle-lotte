Table scaling and size hints

The table is built once at 992 × 900 px and shrinks uniformly to the browser window: scale = smaller of width and height ratio, at most 1.0. Drag the sliders or pick a preset to see how the table, the scaling notice and the "window too small" hint behave. The numbers are real: the table is an actual 992 × 900 layout scaled by the shown factor.

Window too small

The game table needs a window of at least 695 × 630 px. Your window is 1024 × 600 px.

Enlarge the window or turn your device. The game keeps running in the meantime.

What to look at

  • 1024×640 and 1280×650: everything, including the Secured area, fits without scrolling; compare with 1920×950 at 1.0.
  • 1024×600: the scale would fall below 0.70, so the table is replaced by the hint.
  • 768×1024 portrait: the width ratio gives 0.77, so with the rule as written the table still shows (small, with empty space below). The spec text says portrait tablets get the hint; this is the open point below.

Open points

  • The hint names the real cut-off: 992 × 0.7 = 695 wide and 900 × 0.7 = 630 high. The proposed "about 1024×640" is a design target, not the cut-off. If you want a hard minimum width of 1024 (to keep portrait tablets out), the spec needs that as a second rule.
  • Here the header scales with the table. It could also stay at its normal size and give the table the remaining height; the plan decides.
  • The notice sits in the empty header band, outside the scaled area, so it keeps its normal size and never covers the play area.

Fidelity

  • Positions and sizes follow the measured v0.0.5 layout (seat columns at x=8 and x=832, rows 93 px high, dice 60 px, Secured area below). Eight-player rows use 144, 318, 492 and 666 px.
  • Icons and dice are simple stand-ins; the game log is shown only as its button, since it is out of scope.
