    Fidelity notes

    • Colours, chips (.vl-status), spacing and the 44 px touch targets reuse the real Terracotta tokens from styles.css / terracotta-preset.ts.
    • The real app uses PrimeNG (p-button, dialogs, theme-icon) and PrimeIcons; here buttons and icons are plain HTML/emoji stand-ins. Look and feel of those will differ slightly.
    • Seat, pile and card sizes are rem-based and meant to carry over; the oval is a styled element behind a 3×3 grid, no canvas.
    • Timer bar is frozen at 60 %; in the app it shrinks in real time (existing turn-timer).
    • Not mocked: settings/leave/removed dialogs (unchanged), dice rolling animation (unchanged), mobile Variante B.
