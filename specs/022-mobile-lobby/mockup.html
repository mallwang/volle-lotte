What to look at

  • Primary actions (Create game, Join, Ready, Start, Leave) stay in a fixed action bar or a full-width button; lists and settings scroll.
  • Landscape lobby uses two columns (seats, settings) so that more seats fit without scrolling.
  • Switch "Long names + German" and "Keyboard open" in the smallest landscape size to check that nothing important is clipped.
  • Portrait and landscape share the same screens; the portrait rotate blocker only applies to the game table (M2).

Out of scope for this mockup

  • Session persistence and validation rules, live updates, error and lockout messages (same copy as desktop).
  • Spectating, rejoining or ending running games, game log, reconnect banner (M3), game table (M2).
  • Presets and the remaining create-game options (cards per type) are folded into the settings section and decided in the plan.
