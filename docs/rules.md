# Volle Lotte rules

## Fixed rules (not configurable)

- 6 dice. Scoring: a single 1 = 100, a single 5 = 50. Other single dice do not score.
- Three of a kind in **one** throw: 1s = 1,000, 2s-6s = face value x 100 (5s = 500). Combinations cannot be built across several throws.
- After every throw at least one scoring die must be set aside. Then the player either stops (banks the points) or throws the remaining dice again.
- A throw without a scoring die (bust): the turn ends and all points of the turn are lost (exception: Star).
- **Volle Lotte**: all 6 dice score. After a Volle Lotte on a Bonus card the player may bank, or draw a new card and continue with 6 fresh dice; the points collected so far stay in the turn (a bust loses everything).
- A turn starts by drawing a card. An empty deck is reshuffled.
- **Minimum points** (default 350): banking needs at least the minimum in the current run, i.e. since the current card was drawn. After a Volle Lotte on a Bonus card the player may bank at once (the total is always at least 6x50 + 200 = 500). If the player draws a new Bonus card instead, at least the minimum must be collected for that card before banking, otherwise the whole turn is lost; on success all turn points are added.
- Card effects:
  - **Bonus 200/300/400/500/600**: on Volle Lotte the bonus points are added; the player may then draw the next card (see above).
  - **Double Lotte**: banking is never possible, even as the first card of a turn. On Volle Lotte only the points collected since this card are doubled (earlier turn points count once; the doubled value must reach the minimum). The turn ends, the next player is up. A bust loses all points of the turn.
  - **Stop**: the turn ends immediately, the next player is up. Drawn after a Volle Lotte, **the points collected so far are lost** (this is the risk of drawing on).
  - **Star**: the normal selection rule applies (at least one scoring die per throw, more if the player wants) and the player throws until a bust. Setting dice aside only increases the turn's pending points, never the player's score. All points of the turn are credited once at the end, with no minimum. On a Volle Lotte no new card is drawn and the turn ends, crediting all points.
  - **Street**: collect one die per value 1-6, cumulatively across throws. A throw without a still-missing value is a bust (0 points, earlier turn points are lost too). Success: exactly 2,000 points for the turn (earlier turn points are replaced, not added) and the turn ends.
  - **Plus/Minus**: banking is not possible; the player must succeed with a Volle Lotte and then gets only +1,000 (the turn points are discarded). Every leader (all if tied) loses 1,000; a leading player loses nothing; no score drops below 0. The turn ends.
  - **Clover** (only 1 card): two **new** Volle Lotte in a row after drawing the card win the game immediately (earlier Volle Lotte of the turn do not count); points do not matter. A bust loses all turn points as usual.
- After a Volle Lotte, if the next card is Street, Double Lotte, Plus/Minus or Clover, that card type applies and the player must succeed, otherwise all turn points are lost.
- Game end: a player reaches the target score (the game ends immediately), or wins via Clover. The other players are ranked by score; equal scores share a rank.
- Dice and the card deck are generated by the server only.

## Configurable rules (set at game creation, defaults)

| Rule                                                   | Default                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Target score                                           | 6,000 (free choice, e.g. 10,000)                                        |
| Minimum points to bank                                 | 350 (0 = off)                                                           |
| Four or more of a kind: each further equal die doubles | on                                                                      |
| Number of cards per type                               | default set below                                                       |
| A player leaves                                        | a computer with random skill and risk takes over; the player may rejoin |

### Default set (56 cards)

Bonus 200/300/400/500/600 5 each (25), Double Lotte 5, Stop 10, Star 5, Street 5, Plus/Minus 5, Clover 1.

## After a Volle Lotte: which card follows

Only a Volle Lotte on a **Bonus** card lets the player draw on. The points of the turn so far are then _carried_.
What the next card does with them (example: Bonus 600 Volle Lotte with 700 dice points = **1,300 carried**):

| Next card        | Carried points                                                 | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bonus**        | kept; a new run starts                                         | Bank needs the minimum for this card's run (if it is not reached, everything is lost); then 1,300 + run are credited. Bust: **everything is lost** (1,300 + run, 0 credited). Another Volle Lotte adds the new bonus; the player may draw on again. |
| **Stop**         | **lost**                                                       | Turn ends at once, 0 points credited. The notice names the lost points ("lost 1,300 points").                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Star**         | kept; banking is not possible, the player throws until the end | Bust: 1,300 + run are credited (Star cannot lose). Volle Lotte: 1,300 + run are credited. E.g. run 250 gives 1,550.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Double Lotte** | kept, **not doubled**; banking is never possible               | Volle Lotte: 1,300 + 2 x run, e.g. run 700 gives 1,300 + 1,400 = 2,700 (doubled run must reach the minimum, else everything is lost). Bust: **everything is lost** (1,300 + run, 0 credited).                                                       |
| **Street**       | **replaced** by 2,000 on success                               | Street done: exactly 2,000 (even if more were carried, e.g. 2,500 becomes 2,000). Bust: **everything is lost** (1,300 + run, 0 credited).                                                                                                           |
| **Plus/Minus**   | **discarded** on Volle Lotte; banking is not possible          | Volle Lotte: +1,000 only, leaders lose 1,000 (1,300 gone). Bust: **everything is lost** (1,300 + run, 0 credited).                                                                                                                                  |
| **Clover**       | irrelevant for a win; banking is not possible                  | Two new Volle Lotte in a row win the game. Bust at any point (also after the first): **everything is lost** (1,300 + run, 0 credited).                                                                                                              |

The same without carried points (first card of a turn): only Bonus cards can be banked once the minimum is reached. Double Lotte, Star, Street, Plus/Minus and Clover can never be banked and must end in a bust or Volle Lotte (two for Clover). Stop ends the turn without effect.
